Ice cream has a way of getting people out from behind their screens. We have delivered to Wellington offices for team treats, partnered with workplaces on staff deals, and made flavours for special occasions. If you are planning something for your team, here is how to make it easy.

How office orders work

  1. Tell us the basics: date, address, number of people and any dietary needs, through our contact page.
  2. Pick a mix: choose from our signature flavours, the dairy-free range and ice cream sandwiches. Special one-off flavours may be available too.
  3. Confirm delivery: we'll agree a delivery window and how you'd like to pay.
  4. Freeze or serve: put the tubs straight into a freezer, or serve within a short time of delivery.
