How we label allergens

In the ingredient lists below, allergens are shown in bold. This follows the approach New Zealand food labels now use: Plain English Allergen Labelling, which came into force on 25 February 2024 under the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code (Food Standards Australia New Zealand).
