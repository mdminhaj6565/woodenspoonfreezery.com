Freeze Craft is the name we gave to our collaborations. The idea is simple: find a local maker whose product we love, then build an ice cream around it. Each flavour ran in limited batches, so the list below is a record of past releases rather than a menu.

Why we collaborate

Wellington and the wider country have a busy, talented food scene. Working with other producers pushes us to try ingredients we would not reach for on our own, and it gives you a reason to discover a new local maker. Collaborations also tend to become the flavours people ask us to bring back.

Want to collaborate?

If you make something delicious in New Zealand and think it belongs in a tub of ice cream, get in touch. For what is in the freezer today, see our flavours and the limited edition archive.
