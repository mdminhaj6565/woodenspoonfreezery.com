Dry ice

Deliveries may include dry ice to keep ice cream frozen. Dry ice is extremely cold (about -78.5°C). Do not touch it with bare hands, do not eat it, keep it away from children and pets, and let it turn to gas in a well-ventilated place. Never seal it in an airtight container.
