This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used. It applies to woodenspoonfreezery.com. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use the site, such as which pages are visited and roughly where visitors are. Google Analytics uses cookies and does not give us your name or contact details.
  • Messages: if you contact us through Instagram, Facebook or another channel, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name and message. We use them only to reply and to help with your request.
  • Orders: when an order window is open, the information needed to fulfil an order (such as name, delivery address and phone number) is collected at the time of ordering and used only for that order.

This site has no account system, no comment forms and does not sell personal information.

Cookies

Analytics cookies help us see which pages are useful. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out browser add-on, and the site will still work.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Send a message through our contact page. If you are not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Third-party links

Our pages link to other websites, such as Instagram, Facebook and our partners. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.
