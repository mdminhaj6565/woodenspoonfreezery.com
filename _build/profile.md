# Brand profile — Wooden Spoon Boutique Freezery

Read this before writing anything for woodenspoonfreezery.com.

## Who we are (facts we can state)
- Wooden Spoon Boutique Freezery: small-batch ice cream, founded in Wellington, New Zealand in 2011.
- Tagline: **Eat * Good * Ice Cream** (keep the asterisks).
- Past published facts we may repeat in past tense: Movie Night won a 2017 Cuisine Artisan Award;
  Blackcurrant Crunch was Supreme Champion at the 2019 Outstanding NZ Food Producer Awards;
  the Hooray Programme with Kaibosh Food Rescue; Freeze Craft collaborations with Wellington producers;
  the summer trailer at Lyall Bay (first launched Christmas 2019).
- Recipes as last published: custards cooked from scratch, organic NZ milk and cream, mix-ins made in-house,
  dairy-free range made with oat milk.

## What we do NOT state until the owner confirms (Live verification required)
- Current prices, packs, online ordering, delivery days or suburbs.
- Current stockists, scoop-shop or trailer location and hours.
- Phone number, email address, street address.
- Loyalty programme terms.
Write around these with "follow us on Instagram/Facebook for the latest" or "get in touch".

## Voice
- Warm, playful, a bit cheeky, never corporate. Short sentences mixed with longer ones.
- Speak to the reader as "you"; we are "we" (the Wooden Spoon team).
- NZ English spelling: flavour, colour, favourite, organise, chilly bin, biscuit (cookie is fine for US-style cookies).
- Lead every section with the direct answer, then the detail.
- Practical over poetic: temperatures, timings, quantities, steps.
- Safety notes where they matter (allergens, dry ice, food safety).

## Never
- Invent stories, customer quotes, reviews, statistics, awards, prices or tests.
- Copy text from other sites; quote nothing longer than a few words.
- Use AI-ish filler: "delve", "in conclusion", "whether you're…", "in today's world", "unlock", "elevate".
- Put the agency name anywhere on the site.
- Keyword-stuff. One clear topic per page.

## Formatting for articles (_build/content/new/NN-slug.html)
- 1,100–1,500 words (the build counts body + FAQ).
- H2 sections, each opening with a one- or two-sentence answer.
- At least one table or checklist; at least one fact with a real, linked source.
- FAQ: 3–5 questions in the META `faq` list, answers 40–60 words.
- 3+ internal links (flavours, FAQs, other journal posts). Author is always the Wooden Spoon team.
- Dates: `date` = publish day (today, never back-dated); `updated` only after a real edit.
