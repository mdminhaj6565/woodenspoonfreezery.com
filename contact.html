Contact us

Questions about a flavour, an allergy, an event or a stockist? We'd love to hear from you.

Updated October 2026: Contact options updated and grouped by topic so your message reaches us faster, by the Wooden Spoon team.

The fastest way to reach us

Send us a direct message on Instagram or Facebook. That is also where we post new flavours, release windows and trailer dates first.

  • Instagram
  • Facebook

What to include

Allergies

Tell us the allergen and the flavour you are considering. Ingredients for every flavour are on the ingredients page.

Events & offices

Send the date, location and number of people. See office orders and the trailer.

Stockists

Let us know your suburb and favourite store. See where to buy.

Collaborations

Make something delicious in New Zealand? Tell us about it. See Freeze Craft.

Based in Wellington

Wooden Spoon Boutique Freezery is a Wellington ice cream maker, founded in 2011. In summer you can often find us at the trailer, and through the year via our stockists and order windows.
