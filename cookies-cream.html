Cookies & Cream ice cream

Classic vanilla ice cream with chocolate cookie crumbled all the way through. Simple, and very hard to stop eating.

Updated October 2026: Full flavour details, ingredients, allergens and serving ideas added by the Wooden Spoon team.

Classic

Cookies & Cream

classic vanilla ice cream with the classic cookie crumbled throughout (contains gluten)

Ingredients & allergens

organic milk, organic cream, sugar, glucose, trim milk powder, cookie crumb (wheat flour, sugar, vegetable oil, (contains anti oxidants 319), cocoa powder 6%, fructose syrup, rising agents (500,503), salt, emulsifier (soy lecithin), corn starch, flavours), vanilla, sea salt

Allergens are shown in bold. Always check the label on your tub.

What makes our Cookies & Cream different

The base is our real vanilla ice cream, made from a custard cooked from scratch with organic New Zealand milk and cream. Chocolate cookie crumb runs all the way through it, so you get crunchy pieces and soft, cookie-flecked ice cream in the same spoonful. The cookie crumb contains wheat and soy, which is why this flavour is marked as containing gluten.

A flavour with a long history

Cookies and cream is a flavour that began in the United States, and several companies have claimed to have invented it. Whoever got there first, the combination of vanilla and chocolate cookie has become a favourite around the world, including in our freezer.

Ways to serve it

  • Affogato: pour a hot shot of espresso over a scoop. Our guide to making affogato at home has the method.
  • Sandwich: press a scoop between two soft chocolate cookies and freeze for 30 minutes. See how to make ice cream sandwiches.
  • Milkshake: blend two scoops with half a cup of cold milk for a thick shake.

More flavours like this are on our classics list, and our cookie sandwiches are made for cookie lovers.

Frequently asked questions

Does Cookies & Cream contain gluten?

Yes. The chocolate cookie pieces are made with wheat flour, so Cookies & Cream contains gluten. If you need a gluten-free option, most of our plain ice creams such as Vanilla, Mint Chip and Smokey Pokey are made without gluten. Check the allergen table and your tub's label.

Is Cookies & Cream egg free?

The ingredients we last published for Cookies & Cream do not list egg: the base is organic milk and cream with sugar, and the cookie crumb does not contain egg. Recipes can change, so please check the label on your tub if you have an egg allergy.

What goes well with Cookies & Cream ice cream?

It works beautifully in an affogato, sandwiched between two soft chocolate cookies, or blended into a thick milkshake with cold milk. For a quick dessert, serve a scoop beside warm brownies or chocolate cake and let it melt slightly before eating.
