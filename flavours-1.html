Frequently asked questions

Which Wooden Spoon flavours are gluten free?

Most of our ice creams are made without gluten. Flavours with cake, biscuit, cookie, crumb or pie-crust pieces do contain gluten, and we mark them in each description. Check the allergen table on our ingredients page and the label on your tub before you buy.

Do your ice creams contain eggs?

Our ice cream bases are made without eggs. A few mix-ins do use egg, such as the marshmallows in S'mores and the cookies in our ice cream sandwiches. Each flavour lists egg in bold in its ingredients, so open the ingredients panel on any card to check.

How often do new flavours come out?

We are always experimenting, so limited editions come and go through the year. Past ones live on our limited edition archive. Follow us on Instagram for new releases, and tell us which retired flavour you would like to see come back.

Which flavours are vegan?

Everything in our dairy-free range is vegan. It is made with oat milk instead of milk and cream, with no eggs, so it suits most plant-based diets. Some flavours contain oats, soy or peanuts, so read the ingredients if you have an allergy.
