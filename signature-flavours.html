Signature ice cream flavours

The flavours that made our name, including two award winners. Some are always around; others return when the season is right.

Updated October 2026: Award details, flavour notes and links to today's range added by the Wooden Spoon team.

Every signature flavour starts the same way: a custard cooked from scratch, cooled, aged and slow-churned, with mix-ins we make in our own kitchen. What changes is the idea behind it. Smokey Pokey is our cheeky take on New Zealand's classic hokey pokey. Ca Phe Da came from a love of Vietnamese iced coffee. Movie Night tastes like a trip to the cinema.

Award winners

Two flavours from this range have been recognised by judges: Movie Night won a 2017 Cuisine Artisan Award, and Blackcurrant Crunch was named Supreme Champion at the 2019 Outstanding NZ Food Producer Awards.

The signature range

Movie Night

Buttered popcorn ice cream with a caramel corn brittle. Egg-free and made without gluten. Winner of a 2017 Cuisine Artisan Award.

Smokey Pokey

Caramel and sea salt ice cream with chocolate-covered hokey pokey pieces. We like to call it the black-sheep cousin of classic hokey pokey.

Ca Phe Da (Vietnamese Coffee)

Vietnamese-style iced coffee ice cream made with Peoples Coffee espresso and condensed milk. A Wooden Spoon favourite since 2011.

Mint Chip

Fresh peppermint ice cream with dark chocolate slivers we temper ourselves.

Blackcurrant Crunch

Tart and sweet blackcurrant ice cream with a caramel chocolate crunch. Supreme Champion at the 2019 Outstanding NZ Food Producer Awards.

Birthday Cake

Cake batter ice cream with rainbow sprinkles (contains gluten). First created for the Hooray Programme with Kaibosh Food Rescue.

Chocolate Chip

Vanilla ice cream with dark chocolate flecks.

Brownie Batter

Brownie batter ice cream with a fudge swirl (contains gluten).

O'chocolypse

Dark chocolate ice cream with a hint of orange. Think of your favourite chocolate-orange lolly.
