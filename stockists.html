Where to buy Wooden Spoon

Our tubs have been sold through supermarkets, delis, cafés and cinemas around New Zealand, plus our own summer trailer.

Find a tub near you

The quickest way to find Wooden Spoon right now is to message us on Instagram or Facebook. Tell us your suburb and the flavour you are after, and we'll point you to the closest stockist. We are re-confirming our full store list and will publish it here once every store has been checked.

Tips for getting your tub home

Bring a chilly bin or insulated bag on warm days, put the ice cream in the trolley last, and get it into the freezer as soon as you're home. Our guide to keeping ice cream frozen on the way home explains how long it lasts and what to do if it softens.

Frequently asked questions

Which supermarkets sell Wooden Spoon ice cream?

In past seasons our tubs have been sold through supermarkets and specialty grocers in Wellington, Auckland, Christchurch, Nelson, Kāpiti and Wairarapa. Ranges change by store, so call ahead or message us on Instagram and we'll point you to the closest place with your flavour.

Can I ask my local store to stock you?

Yes, please do. Ask the person in charge of the freezer section or the store manager, and tell us which store it is through our contact page. Stores listen to their customers, and a request from a regular shopper often carries more weight than ours.

Where can I get a scoop rather than a tub?

In summer our ice cream trailer has parked at Lyall Bay in Wellington, and we have also had scoop counters with retail partners. Locations and hours change each season, so check our trailer page and Instagram before you head out.
